Masahiro Fujita

Hi, I'm Masahiro. I'm a software and AI engineer working in Security Operations IT at Brigham Young University.

My interests lie in humanoid robotics, robotic manipulation, and embodied AI. I want to develop reliable robots that can work safely alongside people and reduce repetitive labor.

Previously, I earned my bachelor's and master's degrees in chemistry at Tohoku University. My background spans experimental research, machine learning, software engineering, and hands-on robotics, including a national championship in KOSEN Robocon.
